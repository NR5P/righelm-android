RigHelm for Android privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

RigHelm for Android is made by Nathan Rigg (NR5P). It collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app never sends anything about you to me or to anyone else. It only talks to RigHelm stations on your own network.

What stays on your device

The stations you've connected to (name, address and port) and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Station passwords are encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. The app turns off Android's cloud backup, so none of this is copied off the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Network and Wi-Fi: used only to find RigHelm stations on your Wi-Fi and to control the radio through the station you connect to. Radio commands and receive audio go straight between your phone and that station.
  • Microphone: used only when you switch to PHONE MIC and key the radio. While you transmit, your voice goes to your own RigHelm station, which sends it to the radio. It is not recorded or saved, and it goes nowhere else.
  • Notifications and foreground service: while a station is connected, the app shows a notification so it can stay connected and keep the radio's audio playing with the screen off or in another app. The notification has a button to disconnect.

What you transmit is public

Anything you send over the air, including your voice and your callsign, can be received by anyone listening. Amateur radio rules require you to identify with your callsign, and in most countries they don't allow encrypted messages. Only transmit if you hold an amateur radio license that allows it.

Google Play

If you buy the app on Google Play, Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. I receive only the sales reports Google gives every developer; I don't get your card details.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email nathan@nr5p.com. RigHelm for Android is open source, so you can also check exactly what it does in the source code on GitHub.
